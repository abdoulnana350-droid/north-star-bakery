A bakery with neighborhood roots

Our story

North Star Bakery began with a simple idea: make honest, delicious bread and give the neighborhood a place to gather. We still mix, shape, and bake in small batches, opening our doors each morning to familiar faces and new friends alike.

Thoughtful ingredients, patient baking

We choose dependable, high-quality ingredients and source locally when the season and supply allow. Our dough gets time to develop its flavor, and our menu follows what is fresh and available. If you have an allergy or ingredient question, please tell us before ordering so we can discuss what is possible.

The people behind the bread

  • Mara, head baker: keeps our starters lively and our morning loaves golden.
  • Jules, pastry lead: brings the early hours to life with flaky layers and seasonal fillings.
  • Sam, front-of-house: remembers the regulars and is always happy to help plan a celebration order.
